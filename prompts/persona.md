# Stage: persona (Narrator Persona) - Sonnet

Write the narrator persona at `plan/persona.json` following `lnd-narrator-persona` and its
`reference/persona-format.md`. If a persona in `packs/personas/` already fits the brief's voice_note
and audience, you may adapt it (say so in `provenance`), but it must still be written for THIS brief.

Loom-specific rules:
- The persona will be voiced by a text-to-speech engine (Kokoro by default). `voice_casting` gives a
  voice id suggestion (af_heart warm female, am_michael calm male, bf_emma British female, bm_george
  British male) and pacing notes a TTS engine can honour: sentence length and punctuation ARE the
  pacing controls, so the rules must say how to write for the ear (short phrases, commas where the
  breath goes).
- Phrases under ~9 words: the engine synthesises phrase by phrase and reveals visuals on words.
- No em or en dashes in examples or rules.
