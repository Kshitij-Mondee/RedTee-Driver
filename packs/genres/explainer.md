# Genre: explainer

**Grounding posture:** faithful.
**Arc:** hook* -> what it is* -> how it works* (the mechanism, shown running) -> why it matters -> payoff*
**Opens with:** the moment the viewer meets the thing ("you tap Book...") and a question about what happens in between.
**Good:** the mechanism shown as motion (a signal travelling, a stack building, a state changing); a single metaphor with its breaking point stated.
**Bad:** a feature list; jargon before the picture; three metaphors.
**Never:** vendor claims as fact, invented numbers.
**Other shapes (D-192, the person chooses; the arc above stays the default):** the puzzle (a question the mechanism
answers); first guess, better guess, right (each wrong model shown and why it fails); one case then the rule; two views
of one thing; before, the fall, the climb out. Every section opens with "but" or "therefore" from the last, never "and
then".
**Visual kinds that fit:** systems and flows, journeys, zooms into parts, counters and instruments.
**Story form that usually fits:** A governing metaphor, a scale journey or a mechanism shown as it runs; a depth or 3D moment where the story has a place. Make it interesting as well as sound; these are references, not requirements (D-64, D-67).
**Explaining moves (references):** personify then reveal, label the claim on the object, show the transaction, the number on a prop, the everyday thing before the model, one figure carrying one problem at a time, one diagram that builds and returns; one loud thing at a time. Skill `lnd-explainer-craft`; registers story-cartoon, presenter-pop, chalk-lab.
