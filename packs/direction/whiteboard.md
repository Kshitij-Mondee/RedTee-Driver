# Direction card: whiteboard

One hand thinking aloud with a marker, for concepts, frameworks and processes sketched as they are spoken.
Its strength is watching the idea being built on the word; plan what each section builds, not slides.

## Signature moves, and when each fits
- **Drawn on the word** (`B.draw`, `B.write`, `B.heading`): every mark starts just before its spoken cue and ends just
  after it; order the marks as a person would draw: the container, what is in it, then its words.
- **The board accumulates within a section** (`keep`, `ctx.held`): a framework, a cycle or a list grows beside what is
  already there; at the section end it is wiped, and the next section carries only what it builds on.
- **Icons as marks** (`W.icon`): a Lucide icon the hand draws, for a thing the narration names.
- **The one accent** (`pen: "accent"`): a circle, an underline or a tick on the payoff, the only place a sound is worth it.
- **People** come from the designed kit (`kit.peep`), placed, never drawn by the hand.

## Outline (pass 1)
- Each section is one board: say what it builds (a 2 by 2, a cycle, a ladder, a before and after) and what it carries.
- Place the accent on the one word each section turns on.

## Storyboard (pass 2)
- One station per section's board; beats inside a section `continue` on it and `keep` what they build on.
- Into a new section carry at most 40 % of the frame and 10 words.
- Per beat, the marks in drawing order with the word each lands on.

## Not this style
- Never two hands; two faces only (the marker hand and the display face); no line through a word.
