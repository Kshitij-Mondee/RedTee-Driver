# Direction card: isometric

A model of the site seen from above, for systems that live in places: an order's journey, a data centre, a building,
a cloud region. One board is its grammar: hold it, and let the camera and the board's state tell the story.

## Signature moves, and when each fits
- **The diorama** (detail register, about 1 beat in 3): where the system must be SEEN working: a cutaway (`it.cut`),
  a tracked subject (`B.track`), an inset (`B.inset`), focus and context (`B.focus`), flows on routes (`r.flow`).
- **Height cues:** height means an amount or a level. Stacks that grow (`B.stack`), bars on the board (`B.bars`),
  floors of a building, a cloud floating above with its stem and shadow (`z`, `cue`). Use them when the number is
  a quantity of things or a level, not a percentage.
- **Isotype tallies** (`B.tally`): each number said becomes counted units, with the key; units times value is the number.
- **The scale reveal** (`B.reveal`) after the parts are known; open close and widen (`B.widen`), never on an empty board.
- **A second board:** before and after, here and there, or a different system; never more than two in a section.

## Outline (pass 1)
- Pick the system and its ONE subject (the only thing in the accent); write the legend before the boards.
- Lay the stations in narration order, back left to front right; plan where the diorama beats fall.
- Plan the numbers as pictures: a tally, a stack or bars, and what one unit is worth.

## Storyboard (pass 2)
- Hold the board on one station across beats; that is right for this style. Cut to a second board only for a change of
  place or time.
- Per beat, the framing: wide, station (zoom 1.3 to 1.8), detail (1.8 to 2.6) or follow; one camera event a second.
- Routes run along one axis at a time; a route is drawn before anything moves on it.

## Not this style
- People are Isotype pictograms in roles, never a person the story follows, until a designed isometric kit exists;
  at most 12 objects in a close view and 7 pins.

## Motion in this style (D-190)
- Things move on routes (`it.travel` on a `B.route`), one mover at a time; nothing flies over the board.
- The camera holds a station while it works and moves between stations; a framing at a beat's start is a cut
  (`B.camera` with dur 0).

## By kind of video
- Explainer: one order (or parcel) followed station to station, then the scale reveal (`B.reveal`) as the replay.
- Teaching: one station per step, the decision made at the station, the check a fault on the board.
