# Review: Maya v2 from Claude Design (2026-10-01)

STATUS: REVIEWED 2026-10-01.

**Verdict:** the structure is ready for a rig importer now, and most drawing fixes landed. Four drawing defects and
the props remain. Because they come back as replacement files with the same names, they can arrive while the
importer is built.

**Input:** `Maya character design library new.zip`, from Kshitij's `Documents/Characters` folder.

**How it was judged (all by tooling, all from the delivered files):**
- `py tools/asset_intake.py <zip> --batch maya-v2` checked every file against the asset contract (Prompt 0). It
  wrote `assets/incoming/maya-v2/INTAKE.md`.
- `py tools/rig_review.py assets/incoming/maya-v2` (new, D-86) produced the evidence:
  - every pose the delivery's own pose data defines, assembled from the DELIVERED part SVGs in the manifest's
    draw order;
  - the joint test asked for in v1;
  - the twelve expressions, with a pairwise distance;
  - the turnaround and the hand variants.

  The delivery's engine scripts (`engine/maya_story.js`, `maya_views.js`, kept in
  `assets/incoming/maya-v2/_engine/`) supply only the transforms. Every drawing comes from the delivered files.
- Sheets kept with this review: `docs/assets/reviews/maya-v2/` (`poses.jpg`, `joints.jpg`, `expressions.jpg`,
  `turnaround.jpg`, `hands.jpg`, two close-ups, `expression_distance.json`).

## New findings
- **The knee joint shows:** a step in the trouser outline where thigh meets shin, in straight-leg poses.
- **Some hands read wrong at video size:** `thumb_up` reads as a sideways pointing finger; `count_1` to
  `count_4` are hard to tell apart; `ok` and `pinch` are unclear.
- **The 3/4 view cannot talk or gesture:** the 3/4 rig has one hand per side and one face. There are no 3/4
  mouths or hand variants.
- **Metadata:** each file still carries about 8 KB of content-credential metadata; the importer strips it.

## Paste this back to Claude Design
```
Maya v2 review. Thank you: the manifest (z, pivots, parents), the variants, the 19 poses + 4 walk keys, the
expressions and the hair are what we needed. Keep them. Fix these, as replacement files with the SAME names and
pivots, then re-export the .zip.

1. UNDERARM. From 90 to 170 degrees the upper arm's lower outline vanishes into the deltoid and a square fill
   step shows under the arm. Draw the deltoid variants so the arm's underside contour continues into the torso
   side with no gap, no square edge and no lost outline. Prove it with the joint overlay at 90, 135 and 170.
2. ELBOW AT REST. At 0 degrees the elbow fold reads as a round pad on both arms. It should be invisible (or a
   faint sleeve crease) when the arm is straight and only show as the forearm bends.
3. KNEES. In straight-leg poses the trouser outline steps where thigh meets shin. Overlap the shin top under
   the thigh so the leg reads as one trouser leg; keep the seated variants working.
4. PROPS. props/chair, desk and laptop: base slots only (surface, surface2, muted, line, ink, field2), no
   other hexes. They are recoloured by every style.
5. SIDE VIEW. Give the side view real depth: chest and back curve, the sweater's thickness, the head and puff in
   profile; make the 3/4 back visibly rotated (about 145 degrees), not a copy of the back.
6. HANDS. thumb_up must read as a thumb up (fist, thumb raised); make count_1 to count_4 unmistakable (raised
   fingers straight and separated, the others folded); redraw ok and pinch so they read at 120 px tall.
7. 3/4 VIEW TALKS. For the 3/4 front rig add: mouths (the 8 visemes + rest) and 4 hands per side (relaxed, open,
   point, palm_up_offer), same naming with _34front.
8. IDLE WEIGHT. Push the standing idle: hips visibly tilted, shoulders tilted the other way.
9. CLASS. Mark brows class="lm-paint lm-brow" (or lm-outline lm-brow if they are lines), so the contract's code
   catches them.
Report what you changed against each number.
```

## On our side

  it needs. The fixes above arrive as files with the same names, so the importer does not wait for them.
