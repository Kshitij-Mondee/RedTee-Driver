## Open-source illustration and asset sets (usable as ASSETS after approval)
| set | licence | what it is | fit for Loom |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open Peeps (Pablo Stanley) | CC0 | modular hand-drawn people | already bundled (`K.peep`) |
| Open Doodles (Pablo Stanley) | CC0 | loose sketchy characters and scenes, SVG | whiteboard and chalk registers |
| Humaaans (Pablo Stanley) | free for personal and commercial use (not formally CC0) | modular flat people, SVG | extras and crowds |
| Illlustrations.co (Vijay Verma) | CC0 | 100+ flat objects, characters and scenes, SVG | props and spot scenes |
| Lukasz Adam illustrations | CC0 / MIT, per the author | 229+ flat illustrations: people, objects, abstract scenes, SVG | props and scenes |
| Free Gophers Pack (Maria Letta) | CC0 | characters plus full scenes with backgrounds | composition study; Go-themed, so little direct use |
| Kenney Furniture Kit | CC0 | 120 furniture objects, isometric and top-down renders (PNG) | isometric props (raster, so not token-recolourable) |
| Microsoft Fluent Emoji (flat) | MIT | ~3,100 flat objects and symbols, SVG | small objects and props; recolour limited |
| Noto Emoji (Google) | Apache 2.0 | emoji objects, SVG | small props |
| Twemoji, OpenMoji | CC BY 4.0 / CC BY-SA 4.0 | emoji sets | need credit (and share-alike for OpenMoji): avoid unless credited |
| Phosphor, Tabler, Iconoir icons | MIT | icon sets with duotone and fill weights | small UI marks (we bundle Lucide, ISC) |
| unDraw | own licence: free use, but no packing into a library | flat scenes | **reference only** |
| Storyset, Blush | attribution or subscription | scenes | **reference only** |

## Where to LOOK (references, never downloaded)
- Dribbble, Behance and ArtStation search pages for "<subject> flat / isometric / line art / cartoon
  illustration".
- The reference channels we already measured: `docs/research/` (Kurzgesagt, TED-Ed, Crayon Capital, Cleo Abram,
  Lucas's Lab), with their contact sheets and motion windows.
- Craft lessons on depth in flat illustration, for example the CreativeLive Illustrator class on foreground,
  middle and background layers.
