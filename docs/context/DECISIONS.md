# Loom decision ledger (append-only)

Every decision that shaped Loom: what was decided, by whom, why, and what would change it.
- **Never delete or edit a row's meaning.** To change course, add a new row that supersedes the old one
  and mark the old one's Status `superseded by D-n`.
- **Who:** `requester` means Kshitij decided it; `agent` means Claude decided it and disclosed it.
- Rows D-1 to D-21 are also described, with context, in `docs/AGENT_LEARNINGS.md` under their run.
