# What "mature" means for Loom: Kshitij's rating intent (2026-10-04)

This is the quality bar the golden set measures and the vision judge must learn. It is written from Kshitij's own
explanation, after the first judge (D-106) rated clean corporate templates above studio cartoons.

## In his words (lightly edited)
> I want it to distinguish between styles. Don't club cartoon together with corporate and clean. We have different
> styles and different ways of visualizing. But even then, there are bad cartoons for me as well. I won't rate all
> cartoons good in cinematic style. It's not about cartoon but ultimately how clean the video is. Also, for the
> professional and clean style, those frames could've been so much better. I want it to not confuse cinematic
> graphic style with professional style.
>
> Those that I rated 1 were definitely clean, but most of them were looking like a slide or too amateur for a
> corporate video. The delivery is to corporate customers and, initially, their employees. Every video needs to be
> professional level and clean, but not all need to be corporate style.

## The bar, as rules
1. **Style and quality are two separate questions.**
   - First, name the style: corporate flat, editorial or UI, infographic or diagram, cinematic illustration,
     cartoon or character, isometric or 3D, whiteboard, live action.
   - Then judge how well THIS frame executes ITS style.
   - No style is good or bad in itself.
2. **One bar for every style:** professional-level and clean enough to deliver to a corporate client for its
   employees.
3. **"Clean" means execution, not plainness:**
   - deliberate design, polish and consistency;
   - legible type;
   - nothing generic, cluttered or unfinished.
4. **A clean frame can still be a 1.** It is if it looks like a slide, a template or amateur work. Tidy is not the
   same as professional.
5. **A cartoon can be excellent or bad.** Judge the drawing, the staging and the finish, not the genre.
6. **Cinematic is a style, not a quality level.** A cinematic frame is not professional because it is cinematic.
   A corporate-style frame is not professional because it is corporate.
7. **Each style has room to be much better.** The judge should name what the frame would need to reach the top
   of its own style.

## Where this is used
- **Vision-lab skills:** `tools/vision_lab/skills/46_maturity_styled.md` (absolute) and `47_maturity_pairwise.md`
  (side by side).
- **Schemas:** `maturity_styled` and `maturity_pair` both record the style first.
- **The golden set's ratings** are the test. These rules are the definition. Specific frames and his scores are
  never given to the judge being tested.
