## What it says
1. **The kit I drew is the lowest frame in the set**, below today's engine. I judged it clearly more mature than A;
   Kshitij judged it the opposite on all three frames. My eye is not a usable proxy for his (as the vision judge
   was not, rho 0.34). Drawing people as SVG by a model, even styleframe-first and revised on renders, is cut as an
   art source. (Not tested: the Claude Design product, a human illustrator, a licensed pack, image models.)
2. **The editorial register held at 3 on every frame,** level with the studio anchors in this sitting, and the same
   as E1's non-figurative Loom frames (2.91). It is the one path that is reliably "passable".
3. **The scale moved between sittings.** The six anchors agree with E1 at Spearman -0.70 (p = 0.12): the TED-Ed
   greek town went 5 to 3, the rejected office 1 to 4. With 15 frames and no repeat (the set was too small for the
   repeat rule), absolute numbers from this sitting cannot be compared with E1's. The ORDER inside the sitting is the
   usable result: D and the studio frames above A, and A above B. Next sets: pairwise "which is better" choices and
   at least three repeats, whatever the set size.
4. **Not answered yet:** whether image models (C) or designed work from outside the model (a pack, an illustrator,
   Claude Design) lift people and places. Until one of them clears 3, the default for corporate lessons is the
   editorial register, and people appear only where a beat cannot work without them.
