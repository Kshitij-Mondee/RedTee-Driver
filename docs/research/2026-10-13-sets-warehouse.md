| Fact | Source |
|---|---|

## 5. Loading dock

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| Loading bays need at least one exit point from the lower level (two on wide bays) or a refuge; bay edges clearly marked; some edges fenced | HSG136 paras 129 to 130 (read) |
| Dock levellers are adjustable ramps with a hinged lip that bridges to the trailer bed; never at a steep slope; a trapping hazard when the lip folds | HSG136 paras 131 to 132 (read) |
| Prevent vehicles moving while loading: vehicle or trailer restraints such as wheel chocks; traffic lights, barriers or other stop signals; control of keys or the cab; "creep" and "driveaway" named as the two hazards | HSG136 paras 133 to 134 (read) |
| Dock height commonly about 1.2 to 1.25 m; a leveller works about 300 mm above and below; leveller widths 1.75 to 2.25 m; gradient no more than 12.5 percent (EN 1398); bay centres at least 3.7 m, about 4.0 m for wide vehicles; one shelter 3.4 m wide by 3.6 m high | (s) https://warehousenews.co.uk/2022/04/dimensions-are-important-loading-bay-size-must-be-right/ ; (s) https://warehousenews.co.uk/2020/04/stertil-loading-bays-specified-for-prestigious-baytree-project/ |

| Dock lights in two sets: one facing the driver (red: stay, green: may leave), one facing the dock (red: not restrained, green: restrained); wheel chocks are wedges in front of the rear wheels; restrain first, then open the door, then lower the leveller | (s) https://og.mhi.org/downloads/industrygroups/lodem/vehiclerestraints101.pdf ; (s) https://emcinsurance.com/losscontrol/techsheet/loading-dock-safety-tips |

## 8. Palette mapping (our decision, not a source)

The set palettes (`packs/set_styles.json`) have no yellow family. Safety yellow (floor lines, barriers, hazard stripes,
warning signs) is drawn in the brass family, blue mandatory signs in the fabric family, green safe-condition signs in the
plant family (the set contract's rule for green lights), red fire equipment and prohibition signs in accent. The world's
identity says this, so every prompt carries it.

## 9. Scenes kept and dropped (D-156, 2026-10-15)

Lesson moments imagined: a forklift reversing out of an aisle as someone takes a shortcut (near miss); escorting a
visitor along the walkway; reporting a damaged upright; a housekeeping walk; a picker scanning the wrong location; lifting
from a low floor-level pallet; stepping out of an aisle for a reach truck; a trailer-creep check before a forklift enters a
trailer; key control at the dock; a start-of-shift briefing; a near miss reported and reviewed; a shift handover.

Kept: `aisle` (named by past lessons; near misses, crossings, racking damage), `picking` (named by a past lesson; pick
errors and manual handling at the pick face), `loading_dock` (creep, keys, levellers), `shift_board` (briefings, near
misses, handover). Dropped: `forklift_zone` (merged into `aisle`, which is the forklift and pedestrian crossing place);
`packing_bench` (its manual-handling moments happen at the pick face, and a bench is a generic workstation that no
imagined moment needed).

Camera numbers for every kept scene come from `tools/set_camera.py`; the specs and outputs are in
`docs/assets/plan_checks/warehouse/`. In `loading_dock` the tool's "behind" ray for the operative's head meets the wall
plane inside door A's opening; the hit on the trailer's inside wall was computed with the trailer walls alone and is
stored as the four `A_ray_trailer_wall_*` points.
