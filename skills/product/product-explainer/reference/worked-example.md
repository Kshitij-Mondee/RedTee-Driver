# Worked example: a 90-second homepage explainer

The product is invented for this example ("Quillmatch", an invoice-matching tool). Every number is marked as an
example. A real video takes every value from the product truth sheet and its sources.

## The truth sheet (abridged)

| Field | Value (example) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Product, one line | an invoice-matching tool for finance teams | product site, home page |
| The one viewer | a finance lead at a 200-person company, closing the month | owner interview |
| Status quo | exporting bank lines and invoices to a spreadsheet, ticking matches by hand | owner interview; 6 customer calls |
| Cost | the close takes 4 days; most of it is matching (example) | customer survey 2026, n=48 (example) |
| Unique attributes | matches as transactions arrive; learns the team's own rules; flags only the exceptions | docs: Matching, Rules, Exceptions |
| Value | the close shrinks to the exceptions; nobody ticks boxes | |
| Aha moment | a bank line arrives and its invoice turns green on its own | product, Matching view |
| Proof | "We closed in one day in March." Finance lead, a named customer (permission on file) | case study 2026-04 (example) |
| The one ask | start a 14-day trial, no card | pricing page |
| Constraints | no roadmap; no customer data on screen; legal review by the owner's counsel | owner |

## The beats

| t | Role | Narration (spoken) | Picture | Cut |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 to 5 s | hook | "Month end. Two thousand lines to tick." | A spreadsheet scrolling and a hand ticking boxes; words on screen: "2,000 lines to tick". Readable muted. | 30, 15 |
| 5 to 14 s | status quo | "Export, paste, match by eye, then do it again when the bank file changes." | The same sheet, three quick steps, a clock moving. | |
| 14 to 22 s | shift | "Quillmatch matches invoices to payments as they arrive." | Product name and one-liner; the app opens on the Matching view (cleaned capture). | 30 |
| 22 to 36 s | value 1 (the aha) | "A payment lands. Its invoice finds it." | A bank line slides in; the matching invoice turns green; a 2 s hold, with silence on the match. | 30, 15 |
| 36 to 50 s | value 2 | "When your team matches a tricky one, it remembers how." | The pointer drags a split payment onto two invoices; a "Rule saved" toast; the next similar line matches by itself. | |
| 50 to 62 s | value 3 | "So the close is just the exceptions." | Push in on the Exceptions list: 12 items, not 2,000 (example numbers, sourced in the real film). | 30 |
| 62 to 70 s | how it works | "Connect your bank and your ledger. Matching runs on its own." | A simple isometric strip: bank, Quillmatch, ledger, with one line flowing through. | |
| 70 to 80 s | proof | "We closed in one day in March." | The quote with name, role and company (permission on file); the case study date in small print. | 30 |
| 80 to 90 s | ask and end card | "Try it free for 14 days. No card needed." | End card: name, "Start free trial", the address, held 4 s; trial terms on screen. | 30, 15 |

**What the example does on purpose:**
- the product is on screen at 14 s;
- three value moments, each said, shown and held;
- one proof, attributed;
- one ask, with its terms on screen;
- the 30 s and 15 s cuts are planned in the board, not found afterwards.

**What it does not do:**
- no feature list, logo wall or "AI-powered" claim;
- no question for the viewer to answer;
- no number without a source.
