# Genre: onboarding

**Grounding posture:** faithful (the handbook or the requester's account is the source; from_user is material).
**Arc:** welcome* -> day one* -> who and where -> how we work* -> where to get help* -> first week*
**Opens with:** reassurance in the new joiner's terms: you were expected, here is what tomorrow looks like.
**Good:** concrete first-week moments; names of roles (not people) and where things live; one pocket checklist.
**Bad:** the org chart as a slide; values posters; policy recited.
**Never:** promises the organisation did not make; people's names without permission.
**Visual kinds that fit:** a journey across the first days, a map of places, a hub of people/teams, a checklist that ticks.
