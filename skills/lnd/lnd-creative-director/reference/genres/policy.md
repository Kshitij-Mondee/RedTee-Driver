# Genre: policy / compliance

**Grounding posture:** verbatim (the rule is quoted from the policy; paraphrase explains it but never replaces it).
**Arc:** why it matters* -> the rule (verbatim)* -> what it looks like at work* -> the grey areas -> what to do and who to ask* -> check*
**Opens with:** a realistic moment where the rule applies, not the rule's number.
**Good:** the rule on screen word for word, with its source anchor; a scenario with a decision and its consequence; exactly who to ask.
**Bad:** fear-mongering; paraphrase instead of the rule; ten rules in one video.
**Never:** a rule, threshold or consequence the policy does not state (flag no_material instead); legal advice.
**Visual kinds that fit:** the rule as text, large; a decision fork; do/don't comparisons; a scenario with two characters.
