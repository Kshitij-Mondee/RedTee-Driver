# Genre: explainer

**Grounding posture:** faithful.
**Arc:** hook* -> what it is* -> how it works* (the mechanism, shown running) -> why it matters -> payoff*
**Opens with:** the moment the viewer meets the thing ("you tap Book...") and a question about what happens in between.
**Good:** the mechanism shown as motion (a signal travelling, a stack building, a state changing); a single metaphor with its breaking point stated.
**Bad:** a feature list; jargon before the picture; three metaphors.
**Never:** vendor claims as fact, invented numbers.
**Visual kinds that fit:** systems and flows, journeys, zooms into parts, counters and instruments.
